Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026
Developer / data controller: Natthaphan Benkhan (individual developer), Thailand
Contact: Cuphoria.c@gmail.com

1. The short version

Chantha helps you remember when food in your fridge expires. The food items you enter (names, dates, categories) and your settings are stored only on your device. We do not run a server that receives your food list. We do not require an account. We do not show ads and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Items you add: name, category, storage date, expiry date, status (active, eaten, thrown away), and the history of those actions. The names you type are also remembered on your device as your own quick-pick foods.
  • Settings: language, appearance, reminder time and notification preferences, and whether Unlimited is unlocked.

This information stays on your device. We cannot see it. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it (backups you made with your device's backup service, such as iCloud or Google backup, are governed by those services). If you use the app's "Export backup" feature, the backup text goes only where you choose to send it.

3. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device by the operating system. No notification content is sent to our servers. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app or in your device settings; the app still works without them.

4. Purchases (Chantha Unlimited)

Chantha Unlimited is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your payment card details.

To confirm your purchase and let you restore it on a new device, we use RevenueCat, Inc. ("RevenueCat"), a service provider. When you open the app or buy or restore Unlimited, RevenueCat receives: your purchase record (Apple receipt or Google purchase token), a randomly generated anonymous identifier (not linked by us to your name, email or Apple/Google account), basic technical information (device model, operating system and app version, country and language settings), the time the app was last used, and your IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived). We use this only to verify and restore your purchase and to prevent misuse. We do not use custom user IDs and do not enable advertising or attribution integrations. RevenueCat's handling of data is described in its own privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

5. What we do not do

We do not sell your information, do not use it for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and do not build profiles about you.

6. Food-safety notice

Chantha is a reminder tool based on the dates you enter. It cannot detect spoilage and does not tell you whether food is safe to eat. Suggested shelf-life periods are rough guides adapted from public food-storage guidance; they are not official recommendations. Always check the package and use your own judgement.

7. Children

Chantha is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Because we hold almost no personal data about you (your food data stays on your device), most rights, such as access or deletion of your food list, are exercised directly in the app or by deleting it. For data about your purchase held by RevenueCat, contact us at Cuphoria.c@gmail.com and we will help you make a request to RevenueCat. Depending on where you live (for example Thailand's PDPA, the EU/UK GDPR, South Korea's PIPA, California's CCPA/CPRA) you may have additional rights, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

9. Security

Your data is kept in the app's storage on your device and protected by your device's security. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; material changes will be noted in the app or on the store listing.
