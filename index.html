Eat what expires first

Chantha is a simple fridge tracker for iPhone. Add what you put in the fridge, choose an expiry date, and Chantha shows what to eat first and reminds you before it expires.

Get Chantha on the App Store
For iPhone

  • Fridge view with shelves and an “Eat me first!” bubble for the most urgent item
  • A colour bar on every item shows how close its expiry date is
  • Two reminders per item: one when part of its time is left (you choose how much), one on the expiry day
  • Quick add from a list of common foods, or type your own
  • History of what you ate and what you threw away
  • Your food list stays on your device. No account needed.
  • Six languages: English, Thai, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Spanish

Free for up to 10 items at a time. A one-time purchase removes the limit. No subscription.

Chantha reminds you of the dates you enter. It cannot tell whether food is safe to eat. Suggested shelf-life periods are a rough guide; always check the package.
