Support

Questions, problems or feedback: Cuphoria.c@gmail.com

Restore my purchase

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account that made the purchase. Unlimited is tied to that store account, not to the app.

My reminders do not appear

Check that notifications are allowed for Chantha in your device settings, and that your phone is not in a focus mode that hides them. Reminders are scheduled on your device at the time you choose in Settings. Chantha still works without notifications.

Move my data to a new phone

Chantha keeps your food list on your device only. Before changing phones, open Settings and tap Export backup, send the text to yourself, and on the new phone tap Import backup and paste it.

Delete my data

Everything is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes your food list and settings. We do not hold a copy.

Is the shelf-life guide official?

No. Suggested periods are rough guides adapted from public food-storage guidance. Chantha cannot detect spoilage or tell you whether food is safe. Always check the package and use your own judgement.
